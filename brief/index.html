NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang Resigns; Colette Kress Becomes Interim Chief Executive

NVIDIA confirms Jensen Huang's resignation and names CFO Colette Kress interim CEO, opening a leadership search at the AI accelerator leader.

NVIDIA announced on November 12 that co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive officer and a director. Chief financial officer Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor. At a company central to AI computing, that transition is consequential for buyers planning long-duration accelerator deployments and for partners depending on its networking and software ecosystem. The primary facts are documented in both NVIDIA's news release and its United States securities filing. They should be separated from reports and speculation about the circumstances surrounding the resignation.

AI Infrastructure & Leadership: verified source facts

  • NVIDIA's Form 8-K says its board accepted Huang's resignation on November 11, effective immediately; the company announced the change publicly on November 12.
  • Kress, who has served as executive vice president and CFO since 2013, continues in those roles while holding the interim chief executive position.
  • The board appointed Harvey C. Jones as its first chair and said it would consider internal and external candidates for permanent CEO. NVIDIA said its fiscal third-quarter results call remained scheduled for November 17.
Read more: SXF editorial analysis ＋

What accelerator customers should examine

The release does not change CUDA APIs, GPU hardware specifications, model availability or existing service contracts on its own. Nevertheless, infrastructure teams making multi-year commitments should request written confirmation of delivery schedules, software support, partner escalation routes and contract milestones. Monitor forthcoming earnings guidance and leadership appointments rather than inferring engineering disruptions from market commentary. Diversification can reduce concentration risk, but moving workloads across accelerator stacks has nontrivial costs in compiler support, communication libraries, operator coverage and kernel tuning. Benchmark portability before treating an alternative GPU or cloud as interchangeable.

SXF analysis: succession risk versus platform durability

Huang was closely associated with NVIDIA's technical and commercial direction, so his departure creates real governance uncertainty. That does not establish a collapse of the AI supply chain. Kress brings financial and operational experience, while engineering leadership, contractual commitments and the developer ecosystem persist beyond any individual executive. The relevant question is whether the permanent successor sustains long-term investment in architecture, software and manufacturing relationships. For customers and developers, delivery reliability matters more than headlines about short-term share-price movements.

What the evidence does not yet establish

The confirmed event is a leadership transition, not an announced product cancellation or a finding about alleged misconduct. The company's statements about continuity express its plans, not independently proven future outcomes. NVIDIA and its Form 8-K are the authoritative sources for dates and appointments; unrelated allegations and speculative motivations are intentionally excluded.

02 / AI CYBERSECURITY AGENTSSOURCE PUBLISHED: 2026-11-12

OpenAI Introduces Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory for AI-Powered Cybersecurity

OpenAI unveils coordinated penetration-testing agents and a vulnerability-remediation workflow; Red Team access is restricted and fixes require review.

OpenAI introduced Daybreak Red Team and Daybreak Defense Factory at its Cyber at the Frontier event on November 12, extending its cybersecurity offerings beyond access to specialized models. Red Team is designed to assess authorized web applications using coordinated agents that investigate potential attack paths and validate vulnerabilities. Defense Factory connects security findings to remediation work, using agents to prepare patches for engineering review. This is a product-and-workflow announcement, not a repeat of the earlier GPT-6 Cyber model release or the initial Daybreak restricted-access program covered by SXF.

AI Cybersecurity Agents: verified source facts

  • OpenAI's Red Team product page describes agents examining source code and application context, checking reported vulnerabilities and re-testing whether proposed fixes hold.
  • The Red Team page identifies early access, isolated environments, Guardian review and explicit limits on the systems that agents may reach. Its registration page says expressing interest does not guarantee admission.
  • The Defense Factory offering describes integrating findings from security tools and Red Team with Codex-assisted analysis and patch preparation. Human engineers review and test proposed patches before merge.
Read more: SXF editorial analysis ＋

How to evaluate the workflow safely

An authorized pilot should start with a clearly scoped staging application, test credentials and an allowlist of reachable hosts. Require approval for scans that could disrupt service, and log every agent tool invocation, payload and patch. Seed known vulnerabilities alongside clean controls to measure false positives and exploit confirmation rather than counting findings alone. For remediation, require a reproducing test, a reviewed minimal patch, regression tests and independent deployment approval. Do not grant agents unrestricted cloud credentials merely because their objective is defensive. Separate security evidence storage from the application under test.

SXF analysis: shifting from findings to verified repairs

The operational opportunity is reducing the distance between discovering a flaw and demonstrating a safe fix. Conventional scanners produce queues that can overwhelm security teams; agent systems may correlate reports with repository context and propose a concrete repair. But autonomous penetration testing raises its own safety, authorization and reliability problems. An effective production workflow needs constrained execution, auditability and clear human accountability. OpenAI's examples support the product direction, yet neither product page establishes a universal detection rate or a guarantee that patches are correct.

What the evidence does not yet establish

The product pages do not establish general availability, universal platform coverage or independently audited exploit success rates. Red Team remains an early-access offering, and the final decision to test or deploy a change belongs to authorized operators. This report covers the November 12 security tools, distinct from earlier SXF reports about specialized cyber model releases.

03 / CLOUD DEVELOPER INFRASTRUCTURESOURCE PUBLISHED: 2026-11-11

Amazon Launches AWS Emulator for Local Cloud Testing and AI Coding Agents

AWS Emulator lets developers and coding agents test supported AWS API calls locally without deploying cloud resources or requiring an AWS account.

Amazon announced AWS Emulator on November 11, offering a way to run supported AWS APIs on a developer's machine or inside a continuous-integration job. Its relevance to AI is practical: coding agents that modify infrastructure-backed applications can now exercise many SDK and command-line operations in a local test environment instead of repeatedly provisioning real cloud resources. The emulator is intended to return service-like behavior and errors for implemented calls, allowing developers to catch integration mistakes before an actual deployment. It is not an offline replica of every AWS service and cannot by itself certify production equivalence.

Cloud Developer Infrastructure: verified source facts

  • AWS says the emulator uses existing AWS SDK and CLI calls redirected to a local HTTPS endpoint, with generated local credentials and isolated state for each running instance.
  • The initial announcement says operations not yet implemented return HTTP 501 Not Implemented; the system retains emulated resource state in memory until the instance is stopped.
  • AWS says there is no additional emulator service fee, no AWS account is required for local testing, and its published fidelity reporting compares selected API responses with real AWS behavior.
Read more: SXF editorial analysis ＋

Using the emulator in an agent development loop

A team can configure separate emulator instances for each CI worker, reroute SDK endpoints and run deterministic tests against temporary resources. An AI coding agent can create an emulated DynamoDB table, exercise application logic and revise code after a failed request without producing cloud charges. Require tests to assert both success and expected error behavior. Where the emulator returns 501 or lacks data-plane fidelity, mark the test as unsupported rather than silently reporting success. Follow local verification with staging tests against actual AWS accounts for IAM permissions, networking, quotas, encryption, concurrency and service-specific edge cases.

SXF analysis: faster iteration with a clearer boundary

The main potential gain is shorter feedback loops and lower friction for infrastructure-aware agents. Local API emulation makes it easier to reproduce failures and isolate test runs, which can improve engineering quality independently of model intelligence. Its value will depend on breadth of implementation and measured behavioral fidelity, not the headline claim that the same SDK requests are accepted. Organizations should version the emulator in CI and record unsupported operations to prevent tests from creating false confidence as applications expand.

What the evidence does not yet establish

AWS does not claim full compatibility with every AWS API or that a passing local test guarantees correct cloud behavior. The official availability date is November 11, not a later article date. The tool is for testing and prototyping; real deployments still require cloud-side security, cost management and integration validation.

04 / NEW AI MODEL PREVIEWSOURCE PUBLISHED: 2026-11-11

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With 280B Parameters and 1M Context

Baidu launches a post-trained 280B-parameter mixture-of-experts preview via Qianfan API; Apache 2.0 weights are promised but not yet released.

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview on November 11, presenting a post-trained version of its earlier ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. The distinction matters. A base-model checkpoint and an instruction-following preview serve different developer tasks, and publishing one does not imply the other is downloadable. Baidu describes the new preview as focused on coding, 3D design, knowledge work and content creation, with a long context window and sparse mixture-of-experts architecture. It is a meaningful new API model, but its promised open weights should not be treated as a completed open-source release.

New AI Model Preview: verified source facts

  • Baidu states that ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview has 280 billion total parameters, approximately 14 billion activated per token and a one-million-token context window.
  • The company says the preview API is accessible through Qianfan, while model weights are planned for release under Apache 2.0 at a later date; its release post gives no firm weight-release deadline.
  • Baidu calls the model an intermediate post-training checkpoint and acknowledges that some capabilities may fall short while further training continues. Its task demonstrations were created inside an agent harness.
Read more: SXF editorial analysis ＋

How to compare a sparse preview with deployed alternatives

A mixture-of-experts design reduces the active computation per token but does not shrink storage to 14 billion parameters. Test token throughput, routing overhead, tool-call argument correctness, structured-output validity and retrieval performance on actual long contexts. Evaluate API availability, region, request quotas and billing from the current Qianfan documentation. Do not budget for on-premises inference until a specific licensed checkpoint and serving requirements are published. Separate Baidu's curated game and 3D demonstrations from reproducible developer tasks using held-out repositories and design specifications.

SXF analysis: API-first is not the same as open-weight

The combination of sparse computation and a very large context window could make Baidu an interesting candidate for long-horizon automation. Its launch adds competition to hosted agent models, but the value proposition will depend on independent testing and practical access conditions. The announced future Apache 2.0 license may expand options later; until those materials exist, enterprise buyers must assess it as a remotely hosted preview. This is a separate company and event from the November 12 SXF coverage of Alibaba Qwen4-Max-Preview.

What the evidence does not yet establish

The parameter counts, context limit and example outputs originate with Baidu. They do not establish comparative superiority, low end-to-end task cost or that the complete post-trained weights are downloadable today. Third-party leaderboards and price claims should be checked against current, named model endpoints before publication or procurement.

05 / SPEECH AI MODELSSOURCE PUBLISHED: 2026-11-10

ElevenLabs Launches Scribe v3 and Scribe v3 Live Speech Recognition Models

ElevenLabs introduces a transcription model for recorded audio and a low-latency live variant, reporting lower recognition errors across multilingual tests.

ElevenLabs published its Scribe v3 launch announcement on November 10, introducing a new offline speech-to-text model and Scribe v3 Live for real-time audio. Accurate recognition is foundational for voice agents: errors in names, numbers or turn boundaries can propagate into tool calls and automated actions. The new models target noisy, multilingual and overlapping-speech settings, while offering different trade-offs for archival transcription and interactive applications. The company's API change log records model availability on November 9, so the distinction between technical availability and the public launch post is important for accurate dating.

Speech AI Models: verified source facts

  • ElevenLabs says Scribe v3 produced 27% fewer word errors than Scribe v2 on the cited evaluation across 102 languages; its model announcement also highlights improvements in speaker labels.
  • For real-time use, the company reports a significant error reduction versus Scribe v2 Realtime. The developer change log documents a 44% lower semantic word error rate on the FLEURS benchmark across 73 languages, a different evaluation measure.
  • The API names are scribe_v3 and scribe_v3_live. Documentation describes numeric formatting, mixed-language controls, real-time turn prediction and a domain-specific keyterm library for the live model.
Read more: SXF editorial analysis ＋

Choosing speech models for real-world systems

Recorded-audio transcription and low-latency streaming need separate tests. For batch use, evaluate word error rate, speaker attribution, timestamp accuracy and cost per hour on diverse acoustic conditions. For live assistants, add endpointing latency, false end-of-turn decisions, interruption handling and errors on critical terms. Build a test corpus from consented multilingual conversations, including background noise and code switching. Use keyterm lists for specialist language but check whether they introduce bias toward a misleading term. Any automated action triggered by speech should require confirmation when the transcription is uncertain.

SXF analysis: latency and turn boundaries can matter more than raw accuracy

A lower benchmark error rate is useful, but voice products succeed or fail at conversational timing. Scribe v3 Live's turn prediction is potentially important for agents that must distinguish a pause from a completed instruction. For developers, the best evaluation is task completion and human correction rate after transcription, not a single aggregate FLEURS score. The launch expands model choices, but production adoption should also consider privacy, retention, audio processing regions and fallback behavior during stream interruptions.

What the evidence does not yet establish

ElevenLabs' error reductions and speaker-label comparisons are vendor-reported and cannot be generalized across every language, accent or acoustic environment. Benchmark metrics for offline and real-time models are not identical. The release is within the allowed seven-day coverage range but is older than the other four stories; the November 9 API record must not be presented as a November 13 launch.
