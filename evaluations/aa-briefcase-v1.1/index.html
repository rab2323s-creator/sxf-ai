BENCHMARK EXPLAINED

What is AA-Briefcase v1.1?

AA-Briefcase is an Artificial Analysis benchmark for realistic, long-horizon knowledge work. Instead of testing short question-answer tasks, it evaluates whether an AI agent can inspect source material, reason across files, follow detailed requirements and produce professional deliverables.

The benchmark is designed around business-style workflows such as spreadsheets, presentations and written analysis. That makes it useful for comparing frontier models on work that resembles real research, analysis and document-production tasks rather than isolated exam questions.

HOW TO READ THE SCORE

What does AA-Briefcase Elo measure?

AA-Briefcase Elo is not a simple accuracy percentage. It summarizes relative performance within the benchmark's evaluation framework, where the quality of completed professional work matters alongside whether explicit task requirements were satisfied.

A higher Elo is evidence of stronger benchmark performance under the tested configuration, but the number should stay attached to the model setup. Reasoning effort, tools and evaluator methodology are part of the observation—not optional footnotes.

01Task completion

Whether the agent satisfies the requested work and follows the task requirements.

02Analytical quality

How rigorous, complete and well-supported the work is relative to competing submissions.

03Presentation quality

How usable and professionally structured the final deliverable is.

INTERPRETATION

What the tracked results do — and do not — prove.

THE RESULT CAN SAY

How these configurations performed on AA-Briefcase.

Within the same benchmark version, evaluator setup and comparable group, the score is useful evidence about relative agentic knowledge-work performance.

THE RESULT CANNOT SAY

Which model is universally best.

AA-Briefcase does not measure every workload. Coding, latency, safety, factual QA and price efficiency require separate evidence.

CONFIGURATION MATTERS

Reasoning effort stays part of the result.

SXF preserves reasoning and tool settings because benchmark performance can change materially when the run configuration changes.

COVERAGE MATTERS

SXF's table is a dated snapshot.

The official benchmark can cover more models and update independently. Missing model coverage is not treated as a zero or an inferred rank.

WHY IT MATTERS

Why AA-Briefcase matters for evaluating AI agents.

Real knowledge work is not one prompt and one answer. An agent often has to inspect files, locate relevant evidence, resolve ambiguity, make decisions and produce an artifact that another person can use. AA-Briefcase is valuable because it evaluates that longer operational loop.

For teams choosing models for research, analysis, business operations and document production, this kind of benchmark can reveal differences that short academic tasks may miss.

LIMITATIONS

Use the benchmark as evidence, not a universal ranking.

  • Benchmark-specific result. AA-Briefcase measures agentic knowledge work, not every form of intelligence or production quality.
  • Configuration matters. Reasoning effort, tools and fallback behavior can change performance and must stay attached to the score.
  • Elo is not portable across benchmarks. An AA-Briefcase Elo should not be numerically merged with unrelated evaluation Elo systems.
  • Coverage changes. Artificial Analysis can add models or update official results; SXF stores a dated evidence snapshot.

FAQ

AA-Briefcase benchmark questions.

What is AA-Briefcase v1.1?

AA-Briefcase v1.1 is an Artificial Analysis benchmark focused on long-horizon agentic knowledge work and professional deliverables.

What does the Elo score mean?

It is a relative benchmark score used to compare model configurations within the AA-Briefcase methodology. Higher is better inside the same comparable setup.

Is this the full official leaderboard?

No. SXF shows the comparable observations currently stored in its dataset. The official Artificial Analysis leaderboard can be broader and update independently.

Can I compare AA-Briefcase Elo with other benchmark Elo scores?

No. Different benchmarks can use different tasks, graders and rating systems, so SXF keeps those measurements separate.
