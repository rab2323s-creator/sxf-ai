SOURCE-VERIFIED EDITORIAL UPDATE · 2026-11-24

Omni Flash 1.1: corrected serving limits and December migration

Google specifies 131,072 input tokens and 65,536 output tokens, with 3–10-second video output for Omni 1.1. Starting December 3, 2026 requests to its ID route to Omni 1.2 Flash and use its billing: $1.50 per million text/image/video input tokens, $9 per million text output tokens and $12 per million video output tokens (approximately $0.07/second for 720p). The $17.50 video output rate applies before the switch. Omni 1.2 supports up to 15 seconds of video; verify feature-specific duration limits and media handling before migrating.

CAVEATS

What the source record does not assume.

01

Through December 2, Omni 1.1 Standard text/image/video input is $1.50 per million tokens; video output is $17.50 and text output $9 per million tokens. From December 3, routed requests use Omni 1.2's $12 video output rate.

02

Google specifies 5,792 tokens per second of 720p video, equivalent to approximately $0.10/second at the published Standard video-output rate. Other resolutions and input usage alter total cost.

03

Omni 1.1 supports 3–10-second video output and up to 10-second video edit/extension inputs. Its serving limits are 131,072 input and 65,536 output tokens, distinct from video duration.

04

Use gemini-omni-1.2-flash for new integrations; the 1.1 identifier routes to 1.2 from December 3. Text-only calculator remains disabled.

05

Automatic routing and replacement billing scheduled December 3, 2026. Until then $17.50 per million video output tokens applies; routed output costs $12 per million.
