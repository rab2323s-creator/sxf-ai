CAVEATS

What the source record does not assume.

01

The official developer API provides /text-to-video/kling-3.0 and supports both 720p/1080p/4K video settings; product and developer-plan access or pricing may differ.

02

Official creative product reference pricing: 720p without native audio 6 Credits/second, 720p with audio 9 Credits/second; 1080p without audio 8 Credits/second, 1080p with audio 12 Credits/second. Voice control adds 2 Credits/second. These are product CREDITS, not USD.

03

An exact public Standard USD-denominated developer API rate was not established from the official references. No credit-to-USD conversion is asserted and no flat USD rate is published in SXF.

04

Duration is documented as 3–15 seconds in the official user guide. Video output is not a token-generation output limit; no artificial context window or max_output tokens are claimed.

05

Reference credit rates come from Kling AI's consumer-facing creative plan, not a verified USD-denominated developer API tariff.
