AI Voice Agent Benchmarks: Latency, Reliability and Cost — SXF Research

Published November 28, 2026 · No paid access · No primary vendor testing.

Abstract and findings

Voice agents must be fast, accurate and safe, but public studies measure different units. We use three evidence sources to distinguish caller-perceived delay, task completion and speech recognition. This is an original secondary analysis of published summaries, not a systematic review or independent measurement campaign.

Results and sensitivity analysis

Original analysis by SXF / AI Research, based on publicly available benchmark data. Evidence snapshot: 2026-11-28. Published measurements are attributed to their original researchers. Task completion is a different metric

Ray et al. tested 278 tasks across retail, airline and telecom. The published reference text baseline was 85% pass@1. Component evidence: speech recognition

Pipecat's study separates the time to a final transcript from errors that change meaning. A fast transcription service can still feed a slow or incorrect agent; do not add a transcript percentile to a phone-call percentile or mix the two as a performance ranking.

Evidence provenance and independence

  • OpenBenchmarks phone latency and full comparison: self-described independent benchmark publisher; its about page states providers do not pay for placement, weighting or removal. Ownership and funding were not independently audited by SXF. Public methodology and code (MIT repository); the site describes its public API as CC BY 4.0. We redistribute only cited aggregate facts and our own derived values, not raw recordings or full upstream datasets.
  • Ray, Dhandhania, Barres and Narasimhan, ICML 2026; arXiv v1, Table 6 (CC BY 4.0). Three authors list Sierra.ai and one Princeton University. Peer-reviewed publication does not establish commercial neutrality. The selected historical audio-native models use simulated English callers; not a current platform bake-off.
  • Pipecat STT benchmark: 1,000 standardized audio samples, comparing semantic transcription error and time to final transcript, not full-agent performance. Maintained by Daily and community; Daily announced acquisition by Deepgram and a planned independent foundation. Industry-linked component evidence; no unqualified independence claim or copied dataset.

Limitations and next research

These sources have distinct sampled workloads, channels, model versions and outcome definitions. The latency index does not incorporate task correctness or safety because comparable matched data do not exist for all vendors. Coverage is an inclusion fraction; differences in filtering and repeated turns within calls preclude naive confidence intervals or significance tests. Rounded aggregate results cannot recover per-turn variance. No inference is made about future releases, customer satisfaction or deployment-specific bills.
