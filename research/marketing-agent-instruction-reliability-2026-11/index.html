Can marketing AI agents follow a strict brief?

We tested Make AI Agent and Zapier Agents on ten identical fictional company records per run. Both consistently produced drafts—but exact CSV output and word-count compliance proved less dependable. We publish the source data, original operator-pasted transcripts and a reproducible scorecard.

01 / Findings

Output completion did not ensure instruction compliance

All six observed outputs contained responses for the ten company IDs. However, several failed the required ten-column CSV schema, and most email drafts fell below 50 words. The agents also reported no issues even where independent validation identified violations.

02 / Test design

A reproducible, controlled drafting task

Fixture

Ten invented B2B companies with locations, short supplied excerpts and suggested marketing angles. No real leads were researched.

Required response

Ten CSV columns, source-grounded evidence, a short personalized subject and a 50–80 word email draft for every company.

Environment

Make: GPT-5 nano, low reasoning (operator reported). Zapier underlying model was not captured. No spreadsheet-write or email-send tools were configured.

Independent scoring

Exact CSV field counts and header match; whitespace-delimited word lengths of visible draft bodies; preservation of uncorrected source evidence.

Zapier run 2 was copied from an interface that flattened rows. Its CSV structure is not reliably auditable from that text export. Two Make credit figures were captured (2.02 in the pilot and 2.94 in run 3); no cross-platform cost score is claimed. Original transcripts are operator-pasted, not provider-signed execution records. Reuse of conversation history may have affected repeat outputs.

04 / Practical implications

What this means for marketing teams

Producing plausible campaign copy is not the same as meeting a strict automation contract. Verify CSV columns, source evidence, required length and error reports using deterministic checks before relying on agent-generated records downstream. This test complements SXF's separate campaign anomaly detection benchmark.
