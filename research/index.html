HOW TO USE THIS PAGE

How should you scan AI research?

Start with the research question, then check the evidence and evaluation method in the original source. SXF locates the signal; the source provides the proof.

SCAN LENSESFilter by theme
01

Question

What problem or hypothesis is the work trying to address? A clear research question is more useful than a headline alone.

02

Evidence

Look for benchmarks, evaluations, datasets and reproducibility details before treating a result as established.

03

Implication

Separate what the work demonstrates from what it might enable. Keep the original source close for that distinction.
