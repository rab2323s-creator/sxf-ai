COMMON QUESTIONS

Understanding AI research

Quick answers to common research questions.

What kind of AI research does SXF cover?+

Research into AI agents, memory, reliability, system architecture, model evaluation, security and scientific applications. SXF publishes source-backed analyses and links to original work by research organizations.

How is an AI benchmark different from a research paper?+

A research paper investigates a question; a benchmark tests performance under specified conditions. A benchmark score alone cannot establish general reliability, safety or performance outside its evaluation setting. Use the evaluation library for version-aware results.

Where can I check the original evidence?+

Each research guide includes supporting sources, while individual news signals link to the primary announcement. Review methodology, evaluator configuration and limitations before drawing conclusions.
