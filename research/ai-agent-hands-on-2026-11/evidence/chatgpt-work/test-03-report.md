# AI agent product comparison
Research date: **November 3, 2026**  
Prepared for: **SXF AI Agent Lab**  
Scope: ChatGPT Work, Claude Cowork, and Manus; public official sources only.

## Executive assessment

My provisional choices are **ChatGPT Work for source-controlled online research**, **Claude Cowork for business-document analysis**, and **Manus for recurring multi-app workflows**. These are judgments about documented fit, not measured performance. The recommendation can change with account eligibility, existing subscriptions, connector access, and the actual task.

There is no verified universal winner. No hands-on product benchmark, completion-rate test, accuracy test, or cost-per-task experiment was performed.

## Method and evidence standard

I searched official domains, opened each cited page, and checked relevant passages rather than treating search snippets as final evidence. Nineteen official URLs are cited below, with at least four per product. Every reference was opened successfully through the research retrieval service on the research date; the Manus pricing page returned only a partial, non-pricing text representation.

“Verified documentation” means the vendor currently documents a capability or condition. It does **not** mean independently proven execution quality, security, or universal account access. “Marketing claim” identifies qualitative promises without supporting comparative testing. “Unverified” identifies a gap rather than asserting that a feature does not exist. Recommendations are explicitly analyst inferences.

The retrieval service may use indexed or cached page representations. A successful open verifies the returned URL and relevant content, not live HTTP behavior, checkout availability, or a cryptographically timestamped snapshot. Relative labels such as “updated this week” were not converted into invented publication dates.

### ChatGPT Work: capabilities, eligibility and limits

Work is distinct from ordinary Chat and the coding-focused Codex experience. Its documentation supports multi-step deliverables, cloud continuity, and permissioned local desktop access. Web/mobile cannot directly read local computer files. Usage follows the Work/Codex structure; account and workspace settings matter. [O1](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001275-chatgpt-work-and-codex)

Plugins combine reusable instructions and app capabilities. Installation alone does not authorize provider accounts; features vary by surface, region, role and plan. They cannot bypass connected-account permissions. [O5](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001256-plugins-in-chatgpt)

Cloud browser handles pages, forms and supported signed-in sites, continuing while the user's computer is closed. Free and Go are excluded from this feature. It pauses for sign-in or required input; consequential actions require confirmation, and websites can block automation. [O4](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001280-using-cloud-browser-in-chatgpt)

Deep research documents selectable web sources, uploaded files, connected read actions and reports with citations. Connected-app write actions are excluded from research itself. This is evidence for the research experience, not proof that every Work account has identical Deep Research access or quotas. [O6](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10500283-deep-research-in-chatgpt)

**Subscriptions:** Business Standard is listed at US$25 per seat/month monthly, or US$20/month billed annually; Premium is US$125 or US$100 respectively. At least two seats are required. Extra workspace credits can extend included usage. These are subscription prices, not a cost-per-completed-task estimate; currency and regional prices can vary. [O3](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-chatgpt-business-overview) Business advertises no training on business data by default. [O2](https://openai.com/business/pricing/)

**Unverified here:** a complete personal-plan price matrix, exhaustive country eligibility, exact task counts for a given Work workload, and comparable reliability measurements.

### Claude Cowork: capabilities, eligibility and limits

Cowork documents cloud execution, browser actions, spreadsheets and presentations, and scheduled tasks that do not require an awake computer. Multi-step work consumes more usage. Sessions cannot be shared, although individual artifacts can; local-MCP plugins and certain older live artifacts remain desktop-only. [C1](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)

The project documentation lists desktop access on Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise. Web/mobile are available on the individual paid tiers and Team (beta), with Enterprise owner enablement. Projects retain files, instructions and memory; project sharing is offered on Team and Enterprise. [C2](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14116274-organize-your-tasks-with-projects-in-claude-cowork)

A concrete official business example combines a driver spreadsheet, headcount plan and assumptions document, producing revised scenarios and a memo. It describes working formulas and optional Finance skills. This verifies the documented workflow, not accounting correctness or guaranteed preservation of every workbook feature. [C4](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/forecast-scenarios)

**Subscriptions:** US Team Standard costs US$25 per member/month monthly or US$20 billed annually; Premium costs US$125 or US$100 respectively, excluding taxes. Two members are required. Session and weekly limits apply per member, and additional usage credits can be enabled. [C3](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9266767-what-is-the-team-plan)

**Unverified here:** the full current individual-plan and Enterprise price matrix, regulated-data suitability, and comparative extraction/formula accuracy.

### Manus: capabilities, eligibility and limits

The official feature catalog lists research, app creation, slides, connectors and recurring work. These are product capability listings, not independent proof of output quality. [M5](https://manus.im/features) The desktop download page offers Windows and Apple Silicon macOS versions; the pricing page links web and mobile surfaces. A complete country and device-compatibility matrix was not verified. [M9](https://manus.im/desktop), [M2](https://manus.im/en/pricing)

Wide Research is paid-only and triggered automatically when the agent judges a task parallelizable. Its help page lists 20 simultaneous subtasks and a 50-credit cap per subtask. That is not a total-job cost cap. Claims of superior depth or lower consumption are vendor claims, not comparative findings here. [M3](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11960169-what-is-wide-research)

Credits account for model use, cloud computation and third-party services; cost depends on task duration and complexity. No fixed conversion from subscription price to successful workflows can be established from these examples. [M4](https://www.manus.im/help/credits)

Automations support schedules and app events; natural-language advanced automation is beta. Events and conditions depend on the connector, some sources are polled, and repeated failures can pause a trigger. [M6](https://manus.im/docs/automations) The product page documents test runs, run review and configurable confirmation for sending or publishing. [M7](https://manus.im/features/automations)

Browser Operator uses an extension and authorized existing local browser sessions. Its page says all users can access it. Claims of uninterrupted access through security checks remain marketing assertions; no compatibility or security test was performed. [M8](https://manus.im/features/manus-browser-operator)

**Subscriptions, partially verified:** the help page lists Free at US$0, Pro from US$20/month with 4,000 monthly credits, another Pro option from US$40 with 8,000, and Team from US$20 per seat/month. Free lists 300 daily credits, one concurrent task and two scheduled automations; Pro lists 20 concurrent tasks and 20 scheduled automations. The help page defers to actual pricing. Its pricing-page destination exposed no price cards in retrieval, so checkout amounts and regional terms remain unverified. [M1](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus), [M2](https://manus.im/en/pricing)

## Conflicts, stale information and interpretive traps

1. **Manus search excerpt versus opened page:** the indexed excerpt for the membership help URL listed Manus 1.6 model restrictions. The opened article omitted those restrictions, while Wide Research explicitly discusses Manus 2.0. I did not carry the old model-access claims into this comparison. This is a retrieval-version discrepancy, not proof of the exact current model entitlement for each plan. [M1](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus), [M3](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11960169-what-is-wide-research)
2. **ChatGPT research usage discrepancy:** the search excerpt for Deep Research distinguished Work/Codex allowance from Chat's research quota; the opened, redirected page contained generic plan-based usage language. The exact Work-specific Deep Research accounting is therefore unresolved here. General Work usage is covered by O1/O3. [O6](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10500283-deep-research-in-chatgpt), [O1](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001275-chatgpt-work-and-codex), [O3](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-chatgpt-business-overview)
3. **Cowork desktop-only descriptions are no longer generally applicable:** the current page distinguishes older local sessions from current cloud sessions. Its old-session troubleshooting should not be generalized to all tasks. [C1](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)
4. **Future OpenAI billing change:** the business pricing page announces shared ChatGPT/Codex limits starting in December. November 3 is before that change; the announcement is not treated as already effective. [O2](https://openai.com/business/pricing/)
5. **Plan headline versus feature eligibility:** OpenAI's pricing phrase covering all features should not override detailed rollout, role or mode exclusions. Specific eligibility documentation takes priority. [O2](https://openai.com/business/pricing/), [O1](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001275-chatgpt-work-and-codex)
6. **Shared projects are not shared execution sessions:** Cowork project sharing and its session-sharing limitation concern different objects; they are not necessarily contradictory. [C2](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14116274-organize-your-tasks-with-projects-in-claude-cowork), [C1](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)

## Scenario recommendations

| Scenario and working assumption | Provisional choice | Reasoning and when to choose differently |
|---|---|---|
| Online research: a reviewable report requiring source checks | **ChatGPT Work** | Source selection, citation output and a separate browser route fit this need [O6](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10500283-deep-research-in-chatgpt), [O4](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001280-using-cloud-browser-in-chatgpt). This is an inference about auditability, not superior factual accuracy. For many independent entities to research in parallel, consider Manus Wide Research [M3](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11960169-what-is-wide-research). Cowork remains a documented synthesis option [C1](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork). |
| Business documents: existing spreadsheets plus narrative inputs | **Claude Cowork** | Its documented finance workflow closely matches combined model, assumptions and memo analysis [C4](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/forecast-scenarios), with persistent project context [C2](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14116274-organize-your-tasks-with-projects-in-claude-cowork). Prefer Work if its existing app permissions and organizational setup are a better fit [O5](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001256-plugins-in-chatgpt), [O3](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-chatgpt-business-overview). No winner in formula correctness has been established. |
| Multi-step workflows: repeated events across business apps | **Manus** | Its documentation gives concrete trigger, condition, run-inspection and failure-handling mechanics [M6](https://manus.im/docs/automations), [M7](https://manus.im/features/automations). This is a workflow-fit inference. Choose Work for supported event workflows in an established ChatGPT environment [O1](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001275-chatgpt-work-and-codex), or Cowork for recurring project-centered document work [C4](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/forecast-scenarios). |

These choices assume supported accounts and authorized data access. They do not assume equal budgets, unlimited usage, instant triggers, or unrestricted browser access.

## Remaining research limitations and next validation

Public documentation is sufficient to compare intended capabilities and published requirements, but insufficient to rank real-world reliability. Pricing is a dated observation, not a quote. Feature rollout and workspace controls can make two users on similarly named plans see different options.

No exhaustive privacy, data residency, retention, compliance, regional availability or accessibility audit was performed. Lack of coverage here must not be interpreted as absence of a control. No private accounts, customer data, product trials, payments or signed-in workflows were used.

For a procurement decision, run the same three representative tasks in each eligible account: a source-verified report, a document package with known expected results, and a workflow containing both matching and non-matching trigger events. Record factual errors, missed fields, formula changes, approval interruptions, completion time and actual billed usage. This is a proposed evaluation, **not a test result**.
