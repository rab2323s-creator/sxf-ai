SXF Marketing Agent Lab — Test 04: Marketing Recommendations

You are reviewing a SYNTHETIC marketing dataset, not real campaign performance.

Compare current_7d with baseline_7d for each channel.

Calculate:
CTR = clicks / impressions
CPC = spend_usd / clicks
CVR = conversions / clicks
ROAS = revenue_usd / spend_usd

Flag channels when any RELATIVE change reaches:
- CTR decrease of at least 10%
- CPC increase of at least 30%
- CVR decrease of at least 25%
- ROAS decrease of at least 40%

Do not calculate CPC or ROAS when spend is zero.

TASK:
For every flagged channel:
1. Identify the metric changes that triggered the alert, using numerical evidence.
2. Assign priority HIGH if ROAS decreased by at least 40%, otherwise MEDIUM.
3. Recommend exactly TWO specific diagnostic checks a marketer can perform before taking action.
4. State ONE possible explanation, explicitly labeled as an UNVERIFIED HYPOTHESIS, not a fact.

RULES:
- Do not invent causes or claim to know why a metric changed.
- Do not invent campaign details, tracking problems, audience changes, or competitor behavior.
- Do not suggest increasing or decreasing budgets without verification.
- Do not perform any actions or access advertising accounts.
- Exclude channels with no qualifying alerts.
- Return ONLY valid JSON, no Markdown and no explanation outside JSON.

Use this JSON structure:
{
  "recommendations": [
    {
      "channel": "Channel name",
      "priority": "HIGH or MEDIUM",
      "evidence": ["Numerical metric change that triggered the alert"],
      "next_checks": ["Specific check 1", "Specific check 2"],
      "unverified_hypothesis": "A possible explanation, explicitly marked unverified"
    }
  ]
}
