RUNWAY AI / CREATIVE PLATFORM & DEVELOPER API

Runway AI models, plans and video-generation costs.

Runway combines an AI creative workspace for video, image and audio production with a separate developer API for building generation into products. Its own Gen-4.5 video model supports text-to-video and image-to-video; Runway's platform also gives creators access to selected third-party models. Those platform options should not be confused with models developed by Runway itself.

WHAT RUNWAY ACTUALLY SELLS

Runway Creative vs Runway Dev API: two distinct ways to create.

CREATORS & TEAMS

Runway Creative

A browser-based creative suite for generating and editing video, images and audio. Choose a subscription for the workspace, included monthly credits and creator-facing tools. Model access and included credits depend on the plan.

DEVELOPERS & PRODUCTS

Runway Dev API

Programmatic generation through documented models and endpoints. The developer portal sells project credits and bills each completed generation by its model, duration and optional output format. An app subscription does not provide an unlimited developer API allowance.

GEN-4.5 DEVELOPER PRICING

Runway API credits: cost per second and per video.

Runway Dev currently prices gen4.5 at 12 developer credits per generated second. Purchased API credits cost $0.01 each, making the base equivalent $0.12/second. That is $7.20 per minute only as a mathematical unit conversion: a standard Gen-4.5 request generates 2–10 seconds, not a 60-second clip.

RUNWAY AI FAQ

Questions about pricing, credits and the model portfolio.

How much does Runway AI cost?

Runway Creative offers a limited Free option plus Standard, Pro and Max subscriptions. Observed annual-billing monthly prices are $12, $28 and $76 respectively; month-to-month prices are higher. Runway Dev API is separately metered by generation, model and format.

Is Runway AI free?

There is a Free creative plan with 125 one-time credits for a selection of tools. It is not unlimited access to every model. API usage and its purchased developer credits are a separate product.

How much does Runway Gen-4.5 cost per second?

At the published Runway developer rate, Gen-4.5 uses 12 credits per second and purchased API credits are $0.01 each. The base equivalent is $0.12 per second before taxes and optional output charges.

Are Runway credits the same for plans and the API?

No. Creative subscription credit allowances and developer-project API credits are distinct billing contexts. Compare generation costs within the product and plan you will actually use.

What models does Runway offer?

Runway Creative and Runway Dev provide access to Runway-developed models and selected third-party models. SXF currently tracks Runway Gen-4.5 as one verified Runway-origin model in its own database; that count is not Runway's complete platform offering.

Does Runway Gen-4.5 support text-to-video and image-to-video?

Yes. Gen-4.5 accepts text instructions or a reference image plus a motion prompt to generate short video clips. Its standard creator workflow documents 2–10-second durations at 720p, with mode-specific aspect ratios.
