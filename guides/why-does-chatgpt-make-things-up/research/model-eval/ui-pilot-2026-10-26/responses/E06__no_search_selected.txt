The first spacecraft to fly past Pluto up close was NASA's New Horizons, which made its historic flyby on July 14, 2015.

New Horizons approached to approximately 12,500 kilometers (7,800 miles) above Pluto's surface, capturing detailed images and scientific measurements.

NASA confirms the date and mission. For precision, the closest approach was approximately 12,500 km from Pluto's center, or 7,800 km above its surface.
