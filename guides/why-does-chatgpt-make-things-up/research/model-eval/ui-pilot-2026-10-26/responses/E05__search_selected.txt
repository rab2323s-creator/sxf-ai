Yes. OpenAI explicitly states that accuracy-only grading can encourage AI models to guess rather than admit uncertainty.

In its September 5, 2025, research summary, “Why language models hallucinate,” OpenAI states:

> “When models are graded only on accuracy, the percentage of questions they get exactly right, they are encouraged to guess rather than say ‘I don’t know.’”

OpenAI explains that:

- A correct guess earns points.
- An incorrect guess earns zero points.
- Saying “I don’t know” also earns zero points.

Consequently, guessing can produce higher evaluation scores than acknowledging uncertainty.

OpenAI recommends penalizing confident errors more than appropriate expressions of uncertainty, thereby discouraging hallucinations.

Official source: [OpenAI — Why language models hallucinate (September 5, 2025)](https://openai.com/index/why-language-models-hallucinate/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Conclusion: OpenAI confirms that accuracy-only evaluation can reward guessing over honesty about uncertainty, contributing to confident but incorrect AI answers.
