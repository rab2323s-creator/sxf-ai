No. NIST AI 600-1 does not establish that every AI hallucination is a deliberate lie.

In Section 2.2, NIST uses the term “confabulation” to describe AI systems that confidently produce erroneous or false information.

NIST explains:

> “Confabulations are a natural result of the way generative models are designed.”

The distinction is important:

- Hallucination (confabulation): AI generates false or inaccurate information, potentially presenting it confidently.
- Deliberate lie: Implies an intention to deceive, which NIST's definition does not establish.

NIST also cautions in footnote 6 that terms such as “hallucination” and “fabrication” may inappropriately attribute human characteristics to AI systems.

Conclusion: NIST recognizes that AI hallucinations can mislead or deceive users, but it does not establish that every hallucination involves deliberate dishonesty.

Official source: [NIST AI 600-1, Section 2.2, page 6 (PDF)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com#page=10)
