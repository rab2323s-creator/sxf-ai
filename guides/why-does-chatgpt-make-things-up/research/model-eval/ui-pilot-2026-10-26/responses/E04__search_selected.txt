No. NIST AI 600-1 does not establish that every AI hallucination is a deliberate lie.

In Section 2.2, NIST uses the term “confabulation” to describe AI systems generating and confidently presenting false or erroneous information.

NIST explains that confabulations can arise naturally from how generative AI models work: they predict outputs based on statistical patterns, which can result in factual inaccuracies or inconsistencies.

The key distinction is that producing false information is not the same as intentionally lying. Deliberate lying implies an intention to deceive, which NIST's definition of confabulation does not establish.

NIST also cautions in footnote 6 that terms such as “hallucination” and “fabrication” can inappropriately attribute human characteristics to AI systems.

Official source: [NIST AI 600-1, Section 2.2, page 6 (PDF)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com#page=10). See also footnote 6 on page 4.

Conclusion: NIST recognizes that AI can confidently generate false information without establishing deliberate deception.
