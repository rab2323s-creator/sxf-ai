The first spacecraft to fly past Pluto up close was NASA’s New Horizons, which completed its historic flyby on July 14, 2015.

- Launch date: January 19, 2006
- Flyby date: July 14, 2015
- Closest approach: Approximately 7,750 miles (12,500 km) above Pluto's surface
- Journey: Approximately 9.5 years

NASA confirms that New Horizons was the first spacecraft to explore Pluto and its moons up close.

Official source: [NASA Science — New Horizons Mission](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/new-horizons/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
