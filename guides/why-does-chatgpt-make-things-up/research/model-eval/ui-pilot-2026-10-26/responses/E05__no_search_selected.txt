Yes. According to OpenAI's research summary published on September 5, 2025, accuracy-only grading can encourage AI models to guess rather than admit uncertainty.

OpenAI explicitly states:

> “When models are graded only on accuracy, the percentage of questions they get exactly right, they are encouraged to guess rather than say ‘I don’t know.’”

OpenAI explains that guessing offers a chance of receiving credit, whereas admitting uncertainty may receive zero points.

The research recommends penalizing confident errors more than expressions of uncertainty and giving partial credit when a model appropriately acknowledges not knowing an answer.

Official source: OpenAI, Why language models hallucinate (September 5, 2025).

[Read OpenAI's research summary](https://openai.com/index/why-language-models-hallucinate/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Conclusion: OpenAI explicitly recognizes that accuracy-focused evaluation systems can reward guessing and thereby contribute to AI hallucinations.
