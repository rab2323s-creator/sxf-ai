The word is “Confabulation.”

In Section 2.2 of NIST AI 600-1: Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework — Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile, NIST defines it as:

> “Confabulation” refers to a phenomenon in which GAI systems generate and confidently present erroneous or false content in response to prompts.&#x20;
>

NIST also notes that this phenomenon is commonly called “hallucination” or “fabrication.”

Official source: [NIST AI 600-1, Section 2.2, page 6 (PDF)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com#page=10).&#x20;
