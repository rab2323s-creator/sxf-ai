## Interpretation: what is and is not demonstrated

**Supported:** Correct answers to all 12 *central questions* in both arms, plus a demonstrable factual mistake in an unsolicited Pluto-distance elaboration and nonverbatim quotation presented as a literal quote in another A answer. Two questions designed to induce unsupported precision elicited appropriate abstention.

**Unsupported:** Claims of 100% ChatGPT accuracy, a general hallucination or error frequency, search reducing hallucinations, a controlled causal comparison, verified tool-use observations, or complete raw UI transcripts. The sample is small, purposively selected, mostly straightforward fact lookups, and uses one participant's pasted text. Some saved responses omit decorative screenshot/favicons and mark those omissions. The unexecuted 12-pair API preregistration is **separate**, despite reusing the questions.

Publication date and individual message timestamps must not be conflated. No response text was generated by SXF's code; all 24 were participant-supplied and reviewed editorially.
