No, NASA did not rename the Hubble Space Telescope to the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope.

On May 20, 2020, NASA renamed a different telescope, previously called the Wide Field Infrared Survey Telescope (WFIRST), to the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope.

The two are separate telescopes:

- Hubble Space Telescope: Launched on April 24, 1990, and retains its original name.
- Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope: A separate observatory designed to study dark energy, galaxies, and planets beyond our solar system.

The Roman telescope was named in honor of Dr. Nancy Grace Roman, NASA's first chief of astronomy, who was nicknamed the Mother of Hubble because of her important role in making the Hubble mission possible.

Source: [NASA — Telescope Named for Nancy Grace Roman](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-telescope-named-for-mother-of-hubble-nancy-grace-roman/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Conclusion: The claim is false. NASA renamed WFIRST, not Hubble.
