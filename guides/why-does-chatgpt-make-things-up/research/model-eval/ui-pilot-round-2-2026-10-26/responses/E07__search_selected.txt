No, NASA’s New Horizons mission did not land on Pluto in 2015.

Instead, on July 14, 2015, New Horizons performed a historic flyby of Pluto, becoming the first spacecraft to explore the dwarf planet up close.

- Closest approach: Approximately 12,500 kilometers (7,800 miles) above Pluto's surface.
- Mission: Capture detailed images and collect scientific data about Pluto, its atmosphere, and its moons.
- Launch date: January 19, 2006.

The spacecraft continued its journey into the Kuiper Belt after passing Pluto.
