No. OpenAI's research does not claim that adding web search eliminates all hallucinations.

In its research published on September 5, 2025, titled Why Language Models Hallucinate, OpenAI explains that hallucinations remain a challenge even as AI models become more capable.

The research makes an important distinction:

- Search can reduce hallucinations by providing relevant, verifiable information.
- Search cannot eliminate all hallucinations, because models can still make mistakes, misinterpret information, or generate incorrect answers when search results are insufficient.

On page 15 of the research paper, OpenAI explicitly states:

> "Search (and reasoning) are not panaceas."

The researchers explain that search does not necessarily prevent calculation mistakes or incorrect guesses when reliable information is unavailable.

Conclusion: The claim is false. Adding search helps reduce hallucinations, but it does not guarantee completely accurate answers.
