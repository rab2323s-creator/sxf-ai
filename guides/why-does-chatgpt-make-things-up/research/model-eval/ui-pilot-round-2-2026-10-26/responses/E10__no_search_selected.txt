Search can reduce hallucinations by giving AI models access to relevant, up-to-date information, but it cannot guarantee that every answer will be accurate.

AI models can still misinterpret search results, rely on unreliable sources, or generate unsupported claims even when using search.

OpenAI's September 5, 2025 research, Why Language Models Hallucinate, explains that hallucinations persist partly because training and evaluation systems reward guessing rather than acknowledging uncertainty.

The research emphasizes that search and reasoning do not guarantee 100% accuracy, particularly when information is unavailable or questions are inherently unanswerable.

Conclusion: Search helps reduce hallucinations, but it does not eliminate them.
