No, NASA did not rename the Hubble Space Telescope to the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope. They are two separate space telescopes.

- Hubble Space Telescope: Launched on April 24, 1990, and retains its original name, honoring astronomer Edwin Hubble.
- Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope: A separate NASA telescope, originally called the Wide Field Infrared Survey Telescope (WFIRST). NASA renamed it in May 2020 to honor Nancy Grace Roman, NASA's first chief of astronomy, often called the "Mother of Hubble."

The key distinction: NASA renamed WFIRST, not Hubble.

The Roman Space Telescope is designed to complement Hubble by observing much larger areas of the sky, helping scientists study dark energy, galaxies, and exoplanets.
