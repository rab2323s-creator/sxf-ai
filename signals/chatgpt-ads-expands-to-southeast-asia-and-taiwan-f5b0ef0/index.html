SXF VERIFIED ADVERTISING BRIEF · UPDATED NOVEMBER 8, 2026

OpenAI expanded ChatGPT Ads to seven Asian markets on September 23, 2026: Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and Taiwan. The rollout initially took ChatGPT advertising to more than 60 countries. A later OpenAI announcement on October 21 increased the published total to 89 countries. Ads may appear to eligible adults on the Free and Go plans—not Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Edu.

WHAT ADVERTISERS NEED TO KNOW

Eligible businesses can work with OpenAI's Ads Solutions team, agency or technology partners, or use Ads Manager self-service where the billed legal entity qualifies. Country availability for buying ads is not the same as a guarantee that a specific user will see them.

SXF'S CORE ANALYSIS

ChatGPT advertising is a conversation-intent channel, not paid placement inside a generated answer. The strategic challenge is matching useful creative to real decisions while preserving user trust, respecting local market context and proving campaign value beyond click counts.

Which Southeast Asian countries and territories received ChatGPT Ads?

OpenAI's September 23, 2026 news post names six Southeast Asian countries and Taiwan. The announcement also references earlier Asia-Pacific launches in Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea and India. The seven named additions were a new geographic advertising rollout, not evidence that any individual ChatGPT account automatically began seeing a sponsored card that day. Launch list from OpenAI.

OpenAI's updated Ads Manager Availability page lists these seven markets as available for self-service as of SXF's review. The relevant requirement is the country where the advertising and billed legal entity is based. Eligibility to open a self-service account, permission to target an audience and the eligibility of a ChatGPT user to see ads are three different things. A campaign must still pass billing, content review and delivery checks.

Does a regional launch mean ads appear in every conversation?

No. OpenAI's user documentation describes a gradual rollout to eligible Free and Go accounts. Ad delivery depends on user eligibility, location, relevance, policy and whether an appropriate sponsored placement is available. An advertiser can be active in a country without having an impression in every conversation, and a user may see no ad at all. Official user FAQ.

OpenAI then announced another 26-market expansion on October 21, bringing its published total to 89 countries. Who sees ChatGPT ads? Free, Go, Plus, Pro and Business plans

According to OpenAI's current help center, eligible Free and Go users may see ads. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts do not see them. Accounts identified as belonging to users under 18 do not receive ads. Eligible Free accounts in supported markets can choose a no-ads Free option with lower usage limits and reduced tool access; turning off ad personalization alone is not the same as removing ads. How can businesses advertise on ChatGPT in Singapore, Taiwan and Southeast Asia?

OpenAI's regional announcement identifies its Ads Solutions team, agency partners such as dentsu, Havas Media, Omnicom Media, Publicis Groupe and WPP, technology partners, and an Ads Manager self-service route for eligible businesses. These are alternative access paths, not a promise that every small advertiser qualifies without account verification. Interested businesses can start from the official Ads Manager entry point and consult the current country availability.

  1. Confirm the legal advertiser market. Check where the billed legal entity is established; the advertiser eligibility rule is not simply where a customer lives.
  2. Select a specific objective. Decide whether you want relevant awareness, qualified visits or measurable conversion actions before drafting creative.
  3. Match market language and intent. Create clear descriptions of whom a product helps and when, with local currency and landing pages appropriate to each country.
  4. Build honest creative. Use benefit-focused headlines, an accurate landing page and compliant images. Avoid unsupported claims or copying a competitor's brand.
  5. Set budget and measurement. Confirm the buying objective, max bid, tracking parameters, eligible conversion events and reporting fields.
  6. Check approval and optimize. Ads and accounts must pass review; learn from actual qualified conversions rather than assuming a campaign will serve because the country appears on a list.

OpenAI's official creative guidance recommends messages tailored to conversational intent. It explicitly distinguishes ChatGPT ad matching from a classic exact-keyword search ad: advertiser-provided context hints help relevance but do not guarantee a particular query placement. How much do ChatGPT Ads cost? CPM, CPC and measurement

OpenAI's current advertiser help describes impression-based and valid-click buying, with campaign objectives such as views, clicks and supported conversions. Advertisers provide bids and campaign settings; eligibility, relevance and auction outcomes determine whether the sponsored placement is served and the amount charged. This is not a fixed price per lead or a guaranteed cost per ChatGPT answer. Official advertiser basics.

Do ChatGPT Ads expose private conversations or influence answers?

OpenAI explicitly states that advertisers do not receive people's ChatGPT conversations, memories, chat histories or personal information, and that user data is not sold to advertisers. Its advertising systems can, however, process the current conversation context inside ChatGPT to find a relevant sponsored placement. When ad personalization is enabled, certain signals from broader experience may inform relevance. This distinction matters: contextual matching inside the ad system is not the same as sharing chat transcripts with the company buying the advertisement. User privacy FAQ.

Advertising does not influence ChatGPT's generated answers. An advertisement is a separate, labeled paid placement and does not mean OpenAI endorses that brand. OpenAI's ad-serving engineering post describes a separate retrieval, ranking and auction system running alongside answer generation rather than modifying it. Technical architecture.

What can a user control?

Eligible users can open ChatGPT Settings → Ads controls to manage personalization, view ad-related information and clear ads data. On a displayed ad, the menu can offer About this ad, Hide this ad and Report this ad. Turning off personalization does not necessarily eliminate contextual ads. The Free ads-free option reduces message and feature limits where offered; upgrading to an ad-free subscription is another route. OpenAI says ads are not shown in Temporary Chats. Official controls.

SXF trust assessment: Three boundaries should be verified independently: who may use conversation signals inside the serving system, what advertisers receive, and whether response generation remains separate. The documentation is evidence of OpenAI's stated product design and policies, not an independent SXF security audit. Read the SXF AI security guide for broader data-boundary principles.

How does ChatGPT choose sponsored placements?

OpenAI's October 21 engineering explanation describes a separate ads service that interprets evolving conversational intent, retrieves relevant possible ads, filters for safety and eligibility, assesses relevance and engagement, and passes qualified candidates through an auction. The ad service can return no advertisement when no suitable placement exists. It does not replace or rewrite the answer model's reasoning. Read OpenAI's ad stack engineering report.

SXF analysis: conversational intent is the opportunity—and the risk

Our editorial view: ChatGPT Ads represent a new type of discovery channel because the user's needs may become clearer over multiple conversational turns. A traveler might start with an open-ended destination question and only later mention a family budget, mobility needs or a location. An ad relevant to that refined goal could be useful, while an irrelevant placement could be particularly disruptive because the user is engaged in an advisory dialogue rather than browsing a conventional results page.

For marketers in Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and Taiwan, the advantage is not merely regional reach. It is the possibility of showing a concrete offer during evaluation, when people compare options and refine requirements. Yet that is a plausible strategic opportunity, not measured proof of better conversion rates than Google Search, social networks or retailer media. The original OpenAI announcement does not disclose country-level CPM, cost per acquisition, performance or net incremental sales.

Original SXF six-check campaign evaluation framework

01 / INTENT FIT

Is this a real decision moment?

Choose a clear user goal your offer can solve. Avoid generic ad copy that matches no useful moment.

02 / LOCAL RELEVANCE

Does the market experience fit?

Check language, currency, fulfillment, support availability and region-specific landing pages.

03 / MESSAGE TRUTH

Can every claim be verified?

Use accurate titles, creative and destinations rather than exaggerated outcomes.

04 / USER TRUST

Could the placement feel intrusive?

Respect brand-safety controls, sensitive-topic exclusions and consumer feedback.

05 / ECONOMICS

What is the cost of a qualified result?

Track verified leads, sales, gross margin and total campaign spend—not just CTR.

06 / INCREMENTALITY

Did advertising cause the improvement?

Compare controlled markets, time periods or eligible cohorts where a suitable experimental setup exists.

This six-check protocol is an original SXF recommendation, not a campaign experiment conducted by SXF and not a test of OpenAI's private delivery algorithms. It helps distinguish the platform's claimed relevance features from outcomes that an advertiser must prove with its own data.

What would a useful pilot look like?

Consider a hypothetical Singapore-based software company. Rather than targeting vague terms like “best software,” it might run two clearly different creatives focused on a verified buyer use case, use a matching localized landing page and measure qualified demo requests. It should exclude invalid leads, review spend and compare against a meaningful baseline. This is an illustrative research design, not a claim about real ChatGPT ad placements or expected returns.

Compared with classic search ads, conversation-driven selection may respond to evolving user constraints. Compared with influencer or social advertising, relevance may stem more from a stated active need. Neither comparison implies automatic superiority; the result depends on creative quality, inventory, delivery, trust and conversion measurement. Frequently asked questions about ChatGPT Ads in Southeast Asia and Taiwan

Which countries launched ChatGPT Ads in September 2026?

Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and Taiwan.

When did ChatGPT advertising arrive in these markets?

OpenAI announced the rollout on September 23, 2026. Individual advertiser and user eligibility may depend on further conditions.

Are ChatGPT Ads now available in 60 or 89 countries?

More than 60 was the historical September figure. The subsequent October 21, 2026 OpenAI release announced 89 countries in total. The live Ads Manager availability list is the reference for self-service sign-ups.

Do Free and Go users get advertisements?

Eligible adults on Free and Go may see them. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts remain ad-free.

Do advertisers see my conversations?

No. OpenAI says advertisers cannot read your chats, memory or history; the separate ad system may use conversation context internally for relevance.

Can businesses advertise in Singapore, the Philippines and Taiwan?

OpenAI's live Ads Manager country list includes the seven launch markets. Eligibility depends on the advertiser's billed legal entity and completion of required account checks.

Can I switch off ads in ChatGPT?

Eligible Free users may select an ads-free option with reduced usage limits; paid ad-free plans are also available. Disabling personalization alone does not remove contextual ads.

Can companies pay ChatGPT to change its answers?

No. Sponsored placements are separately labeled, and OpenAI says the ads system cannot alter ChatGPT's generated responses.

What does ChatGPT Ads cost for advertisers?

OpenAI supports impression and valid-click buying with bids and auction pricing. Real costs vary by campaign and market; no universal cost per click is guaranteed.
