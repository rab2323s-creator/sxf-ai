How to claim your business in ChatGPT: Hub, insights and agents

SOURCEOpenAI
PUBLISHEDNovember 10, 2026
LAYERTools
SIGNAL SCORE46/100

SXF VERIFIED BRIEF

OpenAI introduced verified business claims and information management, aggregated discovery analytics and optional branded business agents, with narrower early eligibility for the agent feature.

WHY IT MATTERS

Businesses increasingly receive discovery traffic from AI answers. Accurate company details and an understanding of citations or referral clicks can help website owners diagnose information gaps without treating AI placement as something they can purchase or guarantee.

WHAT TO VERIFY

Business Hub and Business Agents have different availability rules. The agent is currently described for eligible U.S. ecommerce or online-service businesses. Aggregated discovery metrics are thresholded and do not expose private conversations. A paid sponsored agent is clearly distinct from ChatGPT's independent answers.

TECHNICAL ANALYSIS / 01

How can a business claim a listing in ChatGPT?

OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT beta introduces a Business Hub where an eligible organization can claim and verify its profile, correct key details and share up-to-date product information. For a store or service brand, this can reduce ambiguity about its official name, available offerings and prices. Shopify merchants have an additional connection path through their store or the platform's administration panel.

However, claim eligibility and approval are separate from being featured in an AI answer. The announcement does not promise that filling out a profile guarantees inclusion, a top position, a citation or more sales. Businesses should continue maintaining accessible official web pages, product data, accurate contact details and consistent information across their owned properties.

An SXF-specific practical test is to check whether the business name, descriptions, website URL and product availability match the information on the company's own pages, then monitor whether the Hub reports relevant mentions or referral clicks over time.

TECHNICAL ANALYSIS / 02

Which ChatGPT business metrics are available, and are chats private?

Business Hub lists aggregated indicators such as the number of business mentions, website clicks and general discovery topics when a business site is cited. A discovery topic is a theme, not a transcript of any one person's question. OpenAI says reports require sufficient activity to meet privacy thresholds; small businesses may not see the same detail as higher-volume brands.

The company also states that advertisers and businesses do not receive individual user conversations or Business Agent chats through these insights. That boundary matters: a metric that describes referral interest should not be marketed as access to visitors' exact searches, demographic identities or private shopping decisions.

To evaluate actual business value, compare Hub trends with properly configured site analytics and conversion events, distinguishing an AI mention from a website visit and a purchase. Treat any observed correlation cautiously; a new profile may coincide with other campaigns or product updates.

TECHNICAL ANALYSIS / 03

Business Agents versus independent ChatGPT recommendations

Business Agents are a branded conversational channel offered initially to selected U.S. ecommerce and online-service businesses. A customer can choose to enter that channel from a business profile, ask follow-up questions and explore products using information the company provides. The branded interaction is labeled and separate from ChatGPT's original answer.

OpenAI says a Business Agent can also be sponsored as an advertising destination. The distinction between paid placement, a merchant-operated conversation and independently generated answers should remain visible to users and marketers. An agent is not permission to present promotions as unbiased advice, nor does sponsorship establish improved organic ranking.

The news value is a clearer framework for business participation in AI-assisted discovery. SXF recommends measuring factual accuracy of the profile, referral quality, customer outcomes and transparency of sponsored interactions rather than chasing an unverifiable promise to 'rank first in ChatGPT.'

LIMITATIONS

OpenAI is announcing a beta and eligibility rules can change. A verified business profile is not a search-position guarantee, and aggregated analytics are not full clickstream logs or users' private prompts. Sponsored agents should never be described as editorial recommendations or changes to ChatGPT's independent response ranking.

PRACTICAL TAKEAWAYS

What readers can check next.

Verification checklist

  1. Confirm profile ownership and eligibility before claiming.
  2. Keep prices, products and company details synchronized with the official website.
  3. Treat mentions, clicks and conversions as different metrics.
  4. Do not present Business Agent sponsorship as organic search ranking.
