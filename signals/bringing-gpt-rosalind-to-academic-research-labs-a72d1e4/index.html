GPT-Rosalind Academic Plan: free seats, eligibility and access

SOURCEOpenAI
PUBLISHEDOctober 15, 2026
LAYERResearch
SIGNAL SCORE43/100

SXF VERIFIED BRIEF

OpenAI announced an academic access program on October 15, 2026, rather than a new broadly released model or benchmark. Eligible U.S. university labs can receive up to 30 Business Standard seats at no cost for up to 12 months, while use of the GPT-Rosalind model is separately subject to trusted-access approval.

WHY IT MATTERS

Principal investigators and lab leads can pursue research workflows in genomics, medicinal chemistry and wet-lab planning with lower seat costs. The operational distinction is between obtaining the Business seats and obtaining approval for GPT-Rosalind. Labs at universities already on OpenAI Enterprise must use their institution's access process.

WHAT TO VERIFY

Only academic labs at U.S. universities are eligible at announcement time. The PI or lab lead applies and passes institutional and use-case review. The 30-seat and 12-month caps are maxima, API access is not included, and OpenAI states research data is not used for model training by default. This announcement does not quantify new model benchmark gains.
