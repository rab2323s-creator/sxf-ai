ABOUT SXF / AI

A clearer view of a
fast-moving field.

SXF / AI is an independent AI discovery product for tracking meaningful changes across models, tools, research and open source. It is designed around primary sources, concise context and fast verification.

WHAT SXF IS

Discovery before commentary.

SXF is built to help readers locate important AI developments quickly and verify them at the source.

01

Signal, not volume

The goal is not to reproduce every AI headline. SXF organizes a smaller stream of changes that are useful to follow across products, models, research and the open stack.

02

Primary-source first

Whenever possible, SXF links directly to the organization, project or research publisher responsible for the original announcement.

03

Fast verification

Headlines are kept concise and the original source remains one click away so availability, limitations and technical details can be checked directly.

METHOD

How a signal reaches the radar.

01

Source selection

SXF monitors first-party channels and established project sources that publish AI releases, technical updates, research or open-source work.

02

Relevance filtering

Updates are selected for practical or technical significance rather than headline intensity. Routine announcements may be excluded when they add little new information.

03

Classification

Signals are organized into Models, Tools, Research and Open Source so readers can scan the part of the AI stack that matters to them.

04

Source preservation

SXF keeps the original destination attached to each signal. The source, not SXF, remains the authority for the underlying claim or release.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

Clear boundaries.

SXF is a discovery layer. It is not a substitute for reading the original release, paper, documentation or repository.

Attribution

External organizations are identified as sources, not partners or sponsors unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Independence

Inclusion in the radar does not imply endorsement, affiliation or a commercial relationship with the source.

Corrections

When source information changes, the goal is to update or remove stale signals rather than preserve an outdated summary.

Scope

SXF focuses on AI developments with technical, product, research or ecosystem relevance.
