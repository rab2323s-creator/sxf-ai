WHAT SXF IS

01 / PRODUCT

Built to reduce search time, not increase screen time.

SXF / AI is a discovery product for people who need to keep up with AI without reading every launch post, changelog, research announcement or repository update.

It does not attempt to replace the original source. Its role is narrower: identify a useful signal, classify it, keep the context concise, and preserve a direct path back to the publisher.

SXF DOES

  • 01

    Track first-party AI releases, research and technical updates.

  • 02

    Group developments into clear intelligence layers.

  • 03

    Keep the original source attached to every signal.

  • 04

    Favor useful changes over headline volume.

SXF DOES NOT

  • 01

    Treat every announcement as equally important.

  • 02

    Present source claims as independent SXF findings.

  • 03

    Imply endorsement or affiliation with tracked companies.

  • 04

    Replace technical documentation, papers or repositories.

METHOD

02 / PROCESS

How a signal reaches the radar.

A simple editorial pipeline keeps the feed fast to scan while preserving traceability to the original source.

  1. 01

    Source monitoring

    SXF follows first-party channels and established project sources that publish AI releases, technical changes, research or open-source work.

    DISCOVER
  2. 02

    Relevance filter

    Updates are assessed for technical, product, research or ecosystem relevance. Routine announcements may be excluded when they add little new information.

    FILTER
  3. 03

    Classification

    Each signal is placed in Models, Tools, Research or Open Source so readers can scan the part of the AI stack that matters to them.

    ORGANIZE
  4. 04

    Source preservation

    The original destination remains attached to the signal. Availability, claims, benchmarks and limitations should be verified with the publisher.

    VERIFY

SOURCE POLICY

03 / ATTRIBUTION

Primary whenever possible.

SXF prefers the organization or project that produced the underlying update. Secondary coverage may be useful for discovery, but the radar is designed around direct source links.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

04 / TRUST

Clear attribution. Clear limits.

ATTRIBUTION

Source claims stay source claims.

SXF summarizes or labels a development without presenting a publisher's claim as an independently verified SXF finding.

INDEPENDENCE

Coverage is not endorsement.

Being listed in the radar does not imply approval of a product, model, company, research result or policy position.

CORRECTIONS

Stale information should not linger.

When an underlying source changes materially, the goal is to update, replace or remove the affected signal rather than preserve an outdated summary.

SCOPE

SXF is a discovery layer.

For technical decisions, implementation details, safety claims, benchmarks or availability, readers should rely on the original documentation and source material.
