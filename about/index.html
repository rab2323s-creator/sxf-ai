ABOUT SXF / AI

About SXF / AI: Method, Sources & Editorial Standards

SXF / AI tracks meaningful changes across AI models, agents and research, with supporting coverage of developer tools and open-source systems. The focus is on primary sources, traceable evidence and changes that materially affect capabilities, pricing, evaluation, safety or real-world use.

WHAT SXF IS

01 / PRODUCT

Built to reduce search time, not increase screen time.

SXF / AI is an AI intelligence and discovery product for people who need to understand meaningful changes in models, agents and research without reading every launch post, benchmark release, changelog, paper or repository update.

It does not replace original documentation or research. Its role is to identify high-value signals, organize them by subject, preserve the primary source, and connect fast-moving updates with deeper reference pages for pricing, capabilities, benchmarks, reliability, security and comparison.

SXF DOES

  • 01

    Track first-party AI model releases, agent developments, research, benchmarks and technical updates.

  • 02

    Organize developments around models, agents, research and supporting technical ecosystems.

  • 03

    Keep the original source attached to every signal.

  • 04

    Favor useful changes over headline volume.

SXF DOES NOT

  • 01

    Treat every announcement as equally important.

  • 02

    Present source claims as independent SXF findings.

  • 03

    Imply endorsement or affiliation with tracked companies.

  • 04

    Replace technical documentation, papers or repositories.

METHOD

02 / PROCESS

How a signal reaches the radar.

A structured editorial pipeline keeps the radar fast to scan while preserving traceability, source context and clear boundaries between publisher claims and SXF summaries.

  1. 01

    Source monitoring

    SXF monitors first-party channels and established project sources that publish AI model releases, agent updates, research, benchmarks, pricing changes, technical documentation or open-source work.

    DISCOVER
  2. 02

    Relevance filter

    Updates are assessed for significance, technical relevance and lasting information value. Routine announcements, low-impact changelog entries and repetitive promotional updates may be excluded when they add little new evidence.

    FILTER
  3. 03

    Classification

    Each signal is classified within the site’s operating categories — Models, Tools, Research or Open Source — while agent-related developments are surfaced across relevant topics, research and guides so readers can follow them as a dedicated intelligence theme.

    ORGANIZE
  4. 04

    Source preservation

    The original destination remains attached to the signal. SXF preserves attribution and checks available source context before publishing; model availability, pricing, benchmarks, safety claims and technical limitations should ultimately be verified against the underlying publisher documentation.

    VERIFY

SOURCE POLICY

03 / ATTRIBUTION

Primary whenever possible.

SXF prefers the organization, research group or project that produced the underlying update. Secondary coverage can help discovery, but published signals are designed to lead readers back to first-party documentation, research, repositories or official announcements whenever possible.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

04 / TRUST

Clear attribution. Clear limits.

ATTRIBUTION

Source claims stay source claims.

SXF distinguishes publisher claims from SXF-written context. A model maker’s benchmark, capability or safety statement is attributed to that source unless SXF has independent evidence supporting a broader conclusion.

INDEPENDENCE

Coverage is not endorsement.

Being listed in the radar does not imply approval of a product, model, company, research result or policy position.

CORRECTIONS

Stale information should not linger.

When underlying information changes materially — including pricing, availability, specifications or corrected research claims — SXF aims to update, replace or remove affected material rather than preserve stale context.

SCOPE

SXF is a discovery layer.

For implementation decisions, pricing, model specifications, benchmarks, security guidance, safety claims or availability, readers should verify the latest original documentation and source material.
